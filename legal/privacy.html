Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Ball Arena connects to TikTok so you can upload videos you create with the tool.

What we access

  • Basic TikTok account info needed to confirm you are signed in.
  • Permission to upload/publish videos you choose to post.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your TikTok access to post content without your action in Ball Arena / Slack.
  • We do not collect personal data beyond what is needed to authenticate and upload.

Tokens and credentials are stored locally for your own use of the tool. You can revoke access anytime in TikTok settings.
